# Project 9 Reflection – Norse Realms Living Poster

## Gen AI Usage

For this project, I used Claude as a coding assistant and for early brainstorming. I came into it with a pretty open mind, and through that process I landed on the Nine Realms of Norse mythology as a concept. That ended up fitting the “living poster” format really well and gave me a strong theme to build around.

## Development Process

From there, most of the direction was on me. I built the project using my existing Gibson Media site structure, so I had to make sure everything matched the navigation, footer, typography, and overall design language I’ve already been using.

I also sourced all of the images myself and made decisions about what actually fit the tone and lore of each realm. For example, I replaced the original Niflheim image when it didn’t feel right, and I caught that the Yggdrasil image hadn't been accounted for in the JavaScript before it was written.

## Problem Solving & Debugging

A big part of this project ended up being debugging and refining the experience. I ran into a number of issues, including a black screen on load, conflicting CSS rules that broke the layout, and shapes not rendering properly due to display vs opacity conflicts.

To fix these, I used the browser console to track errors and worked through them step by step. This process involved adjusting both the structure and the logic until everything behaved correctly. I also ran into a Cloudflare email obfuscation issue, which I had to identify and work around.

## Reflection

Overall, it felt more like directing the project and refining it as I went. I made the creative decisions, handled troubleshooting, and adjusted things until the final result matched what I had in mind.

I’m really happy with how it turned out, especially in terms of how the visuals and transitions came together. It feels cohesive with the rest of my site and works the way I originally envisioned.
